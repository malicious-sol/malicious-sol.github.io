Privacy policy

Malicious Hub is a personal app used only by its owner's household. It isn't offered to the public.

What it reads from your Google account

  • Google Calendar (read only): to show your events.
  • Google Tasks: to show, add and tick off your to-do items.
  • Google Drive, the app's own hidden folder only: to keep the Focus timer's history in step across your PCs. It can't see your other Drive files.
  • Your email address: to show which account is signed in.

Where it goes

This information stays on your own devices (your PC and your box at home) and in your own Google account. It isn't sent to anybody else, sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no servers run by the app.

Stopping it

Sign out in the app (Setup > Google), or remove Malicious Hub's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
